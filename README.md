### Features

- **Synchronized Lyrics:** Word-by-word and line-by-line synced lyrics that automatically scroll with the music.
- **Smart Romanization:** Instant transliteration for Japanese (Kanji/Kana to Romaji), Korean (Hangul to Romanized), and Indic scripts (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bengali, Punjabi, etc.).
- **Local-First & Offline:** Embedded encrypted SQLite database keeps your library, favorites, and playlists strictly on your machine.
- **100% Free & Private:** Zero ads, zero tracking, zero subscriptions, and no account required.
- **Cross-Platform:** Available on Windows 10/11 and Android 8.0+.
